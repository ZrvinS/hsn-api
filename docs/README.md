# HSN Rate API: documentation index

HSN Rate API (`hsn-api`) is one service that lets any billing app (Sunarone, the health app, retailers) look up GST rate rules by HSN/SAC code, get best-guess HSN suggestions from a product name or barcode, calculate CGST/SGST/IGST, and follow a change feed. Rates are curated by people from CBIC notifications and versioned by effective date. Every rate carries a citation. The service is **not** an official or real-time government feed.

Status as of 2026-10-11: the documents are drafts; the server runs and its suite passes. **No rate is verified yet**: the shipped seed has 356 HSN/SAC codes and 70 active rules, all `unverified`, and the classifier's eval labels are LLM drafts pending human review. The 28 cross-document inconsistencies formerly listed in TRD §20 were settled by [00b-coordinator-rulings.md](00b-coordinator-rulings.md), and the round-1 validator and security findings by [00c-coordinator-rulings-round2.md](00c-coordinator-rulings-round2.md) (rulings 1–35), and ruling 36 (2026-10-11, in [00-coordinator-decisions.md](00-coordinator-decisions.md)); all are applied across the docs. PRD §10 lists open questions for Amit, the most important being Q1 (how making charges are treated at Sunarone) and Q3 (who the second reviewer is).
